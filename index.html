Who We Are

Where DAVIANS
Become Innovators

HackLab is a student-run innovation community inside our school that bridges the gap between learning and creating. We believe every student has the potential to build something amazing.

From your very first line of code to launching a full project — HackLab provides the knowledge, mentorship, and collaborative environment to make it happen. All resources are completely free.

Whether you're into web development, AI, mobile apps, or IoT — there's a place for you here. Ask questions, share ideas, and grow together with a community that gets it.

Your Journey

From Zero to Innovator

A clear roadmap guiding you from absolute beginner to building impactful projects.

01
Stage 1

🌱 Beginner

Start with the basics — HTML, CSS, Python fundamentals. Build your first webpage and write your first programs with guided support.

02
Stage 2

🔍 Explorer

Dive deeper into JavaScript, APIs, databases, and version control. Start collaborating on small group projects.

03
Stage 3

🔨 Builder

Take on real projects — build full-stack apps, contribute to open source, and participate in hackathons with your team.

04
Stage 4

🚀 Innovator

Lead projects, mentor newcomers, and launch solutions that solve real problems. You're now part of the next generation of innovators.
